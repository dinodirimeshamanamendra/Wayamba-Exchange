Three ways to exchange

Every post is either Giving or Requesting, and is marked with one of these item types.

Exchange

Swap one item for another

Offer something you have in return for something you need, with no money involved.

Sale

Sell at a fair student price

Set a price for textbooks, equipment and other things you are finished with.

Free

Give it to someone who needs it

Pass on items at no cost, such as notes, lab coats and kitchen items.

About us

Wayamba Exchange is a student platform for Wayamba University of Sri Lanka. Students often finish with textbooks, calculators, stationery and hostel items they no longer need, while new students buy the same things at full price.

This website brings giving, requesting, exchanging and selling into one place, so items are reused and students save time and money.
